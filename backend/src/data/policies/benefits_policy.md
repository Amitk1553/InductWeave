# Employee Benefits Policy

Nimbus Technologies offers a comprehensive, market-leading benefits program designed to support the health, wellness, and financial security of our employees and their families across all global locations.

## 5.1 Benefits Program Overview

Our benefits package is an integral component of your total compensation at Nimbus Technologies. Benefits eligibility begins on your very first day of employment for all regular full-time employees. Coverage options and provider networks vary by geographical location to align with local healthcare systems and regulatory standards, but the core principles of quality care, comprehensive protection, and employer sponsorship remain consistent worldwide.

## 5.2 Health Insurance Enrollment Window

Medical, dental, and vision insurance coverage requires timely election by newly joined personnel:
- Enrollment Window: The health insurance enrollment window is strictly within thirty (30) calendar days of joining Nimbus Technologies.
- Coverage Start Date: Insurance coverage becomes effective retroactively to the employee's official start date, or on the first day of the calendar month following the joining date according to the specific plan chosen in the benefits portal.
- Default Inaction: If an employee does not complete benefits enrollment within the 30-day window, they are automatically enrolled in default individual-only catastrophic coverage and cannot make plan changes until the next annual Open Enrollment period, unless a qualifying life event occurs (such as marriage, birth of a child, or loss of spouse coverage).

## 5.3 Step-by-Step Health Insurance Enrollment

Employees must complete their benefits selections digitally via the Nimbus Benefits Portal. Follow these steps to enroll:
1. Log in to the Nimbus Employee Portal using your Okta SSO credentials and navigate to the "Benefits & Wellbeing" tab.
2. Select "New Hire Enrollment" and review available medical, dental, and vision health plan tiers along with deductibles, co-pays, and out-of-pocket maximums.
3. Add eligible dependent details (legal name, date of birth, government identification number, and relationship documentation).
4. Select primary and secondary beneficiaries for group term life and accidental death coverage.
5. Review your total monthly payroll deductions summary and submit your digital election confirmation. You will receive an automated email receipt with your policy confirmation number.

## 5.4 Eligible Dependents Coverage

Nimbus Technologies provides group health coverage options for eligible family members of enrolled employees:
- Spouse or Domestic Partner: Legal spouses and registered same-sex or opposite-sex domestic partners.
- Dependent Children: Biological children, legally adopted children, stepchildren, and children under legal guardianship up to age 26, regardless of student or marital status.
- Dependent Parents (India only): Under the India group medical scheme, employees may enroll dependent parents or parents-in-law up to the designated sum insured.
- Verification Documentation: Proof of dependency (marriage certificate, birth certificate, or legal adoption decree) must be uploaded within fifteen (15) days of benefits submission.

## 5.5 Wellness and Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

Mental and physical wellbeing is supported through global corporate wellness initiatives:
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): Nimbus provides a 24/7 confidential Employee Assistance Program offering up to eight (8) free counseling sessions per issue per year for employees and household family members.
- Wellness Allowance: Full-time employees are eligible for an annual wellness stipend of $600 USD (or local currency equivalent) to reimburse gym memberships, fitness equipment, mindfulness apps, or ergonomic office gear.
- Annual Health Screenings: Nimbus sponsors optional annual biometric health screenings and on-site or voucher-based seasonal flu vaccinations.

## 5.6 Additional Company Perks and Allowances

To foster ongoing professional growth and comfortable working environments, Nimbus provides several additional global benefits:
- Learning & Development Stipend: Every employee is allocated an annual learning budget of $1,000 USD (or local equivalent) for conferences, professional certifications, books, and educational courses approved by their manager.
- Home Office Setup Allowance: A one-time net stipend of $500 USD is provided to hybrid and remote employees during their first month to purchase external monitors, ergonomic chairs, and desk accessories.
- Parental Leave: Nimbus provides 16 weeks of gender-neutral paid parental leave for primary caregivers welcoming a new child through birth, adoption, or foster placement.
