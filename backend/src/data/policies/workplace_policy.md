# Workplace and Conduct Policy

This policy outlines the expected workplace standards, working arrangements, leave policies, general company policies, and professional conduct principles for all employees of Nimbus Technologies worldwide.

## 6.1 Workplace Philosophy and General Company Policies

Nimbus Technologies is committed to providing an ethical, transparent, inclusive, and high-performance work environment across all global locations in Mumbai, Bangalore, New York, London, and Toronto. Our general company policies apply to all full-time employees, part-time personnel, contractors, and interns:
- Equal Opportunity and Mutual Respect: We maintain an open, inclusive culture founded on integrity, personal dignity, and psychological safety. Discrimination, harassment, or retaliation of any nature is strictly prohibited.
- Standards of Conduct: All personnel are expected to act with honesty, protect intellectual property, safeguard customer and company confidential data, and avoid personal conflicts of interest.
- Core Policy Directory: Employees are guided by established corporate policies covering Onboarding, IT Equipment and Cybersecurity, Health and Wellness Benefits, Remote and Hybrid Work Flexibility, Paid Time Off, and Code of Conduct.
- Living Guidelines: Detailed operational procedures are documented in the Nimbus Employee Portal. Policy guidelines are reviewed on an ongoing basis by People Operations, and new policy modules or procedural updates are added regularly to support our global team.

## 6.2 Dress Code Policy

Nimbus Technologies maintains a professional yet comfortable atmosphere appropriate for a contemporary technology company:
- Standard Dress Code: The standard company dress code from Monday through Thursday is business casual. Acceptable attire includes collared shirts, blouses, sweaters, dark denim jeans without rips, tailored trousers, chinos, dresses, and clean closed-toe shoes or smart sneakers.
- Relaxed Fridays: On Fridays, the dress code is relaxed casual. Employees may wear casual t-shirts (free from offensive language or political slogans), relaxed-fit jeans, hoodies, and casual footwear.
- Client and External Meetings: When attending formal client meetings, customer presentations, industry conferences, or regulatory hearings, employees are expected to wear formal business attire (suits, blazers, dress shirts, and formal business shoes).
- Prohibited Attire: Clothing that is revealing, beachwear, athletic workout clothes (sweatpants, gym shorts), flip-flops, or apparel bearing discriminatory imagery or slogans is strictly prohibited in all office locations.

## 6.3 Working Hours and Core Collaboration Windows

To maintain cross-team productivity while respecting personal schedules, Nimbus Technologies implements flexible working hours built around core collaboration periods:
- Standard Workweek: Full-time employment contracts are based on a forty (40) hour workweek, typically Monday through Friday.
- Core Collaboration Windows: All team members must be online, reachable, and available for meetings during core collaboration hours between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM in their primary office time zone.
- Flexible Hours: Outside of core collaboration hours, employees may adjust their starting and ending hours with mutual agreement from their direct manager, provided their 40-hour weekly commitment is fulfilled.

## 6.4 Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Nimbus Technologies supports modern hybrid working models that balance in-person collaboration with personal flexibility:
- Hybrid Schedule: Most engineering, product, and business operations roles follow a hybrid arrangement requiring three (3) days in the local office and two (2) days working remotely from home per week.
- Office Days: Department managers coordinate designated team office days (typically Tuesday through Thursday) to ensure effective in-person collaboration and team brainstorming.
- Full-Remote Employees: Full-remote status requires formal approval from the Department VP and People Operations during the hiring process or through an exceptional remote work request.
- Ergonomics and Security: Remote workspaces must maintain quiet environments suitable for video conferencing and comply with corporate network security policies, including active VPN usage.

## 6.5 Paid Time Off and Leave Policy

Maintaining a healthy work-life balance is essential for long-term sustained creativity and employee health:
- Annual Paid Vacation: Full-time employees accrue twenty (20) days of paid annual vacation leave per calendar year, accrued monthly on a pro-rata basis.
- Sick and Personal Leave: Employees receive ten (10) days of paid sick and casual leave per year for personal illness, medical appointments, or family emergencies.
- Leave Approvals: Vacation requests of more than three consecutive days must be submitted in the HR portal at least two (2) weeks in advance and approved by the direct manager.
- Carryover Limit: A maximum of five (5) accrued unused vacation days may be carried over into the following calendar year, expiring on March 31st if unused.

## 6.6 Company Holidays

Nimbus Technologies recognizes standard public and national holidays across each of our operating locations:
- Each regional office observes ten (10) official paid public holidays per calendar year based on the state or national holiday calendar published annually by HR.
- In addition to standard public holidays, all employees receive two (2) floating holidays per calendar year to observe cultural, religious, or personal days of significance.
- When an official public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, it is observed on the preceding Friday or following Monday in accordance with local statutory custom.

## 6.7 Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment

Nimbus Technologies is committed to maintaining a workplace completely free from harassment, discrimination, and retaliation:
- Zero Tolerance: We have a strict zero-tolerance policy for discrimination or harassment based on race, color, religion, gender, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, or genetic information.
- Professional Boundaries: Romantic relationships between direct supervisors and reporting subordinates must be disclosed immediately to People Operations to eliminate conflicts of interest.
- Reporting Concerns: Employees who experience or witness unethical conduct, harassment, or security violations can report incidents directly to their manager, People Operations, or confidentially via the Nimbus Ethics Hotline at ethics@nimbus.example.
- Non-Retaliation: Retaliation against any individual who reports a concern or participates in an investigation in good faith is illegal and results in immediate termination of employment.
