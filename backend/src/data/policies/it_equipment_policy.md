# IT and Equipment Policy

This policy applies to all employees and describes how equipment and access are requested and provisioned. For help at any time, contact the IT Service Desk at itsupport@company.example or through the #it-help channel.

## Laptop Request Lead Time

Laptops are ordered by IT after the employment contract is signed. A laptop request needs at least 5 business days of lead time before the start date; requests made with less notice cannot be guaranteed and a temporary loaner may be issued. Standard configurations are a 14-inch MacBook Pro for engineering roles and a standard business laptop for all other roles. Requests for non-standard hardware require manager and IT approval and add up to two weeks. The laptop is encrypted and enrolled in device management before shipping; new hires in offices collect it from the IT desk on day one, remote employees receive it by courier. For laptop setup issues, contact the IT Service Desk.

## Account Creation and Single Sign-On

IT creates your company email address and single sign-on (SSO) identity at least three days before your start date, once HR confirms the signed contract. You receive a temporary password and must register multi-factor authentication (MFA) on your first login; accounts without MFA are suspended after seven days. The SSO account gives you access to email, calendar, the chat tool, the HR portal and the benefits portal. Do not share your credentials with anyone, including IT staff.

## VPN Setup

VPN setup must be completed before you can access any internal system, such as internal wikis, the staging environment or finance tools. IT installs the VPN client on your laptop during provisioning and you verify the connection on your first day. If the VPN fails to connect, raise a ticket marked "VPN" with the IT Service Desk. The VPN must be used whenever you work from public networks, and split tunnelling is disabled for engineering and finance roles.

## Source Code and Cloud Access

Engineering employees receive access to the GitHub organisation, the team repositories and the cloud console following three conditions: the account is created, the background check has been cleared, and the VPN is working. Access is least-privilege: new engineers receive read-write access to their team repositories and read-only access to the staging cloud account. Production access requires completion of the security training and manager approval, and is reviewed every 90 days. All code changes require peer review before merging.

## Equipment Return and Loss

Company equipment remains company property. Report lost or stolen devices to the IT Service Desk and your manager within 24 hours so the device can be remotely locked and wiped. On leaving the company, equipment must be returned within five business days; a prepaid courier box is provided to remote employees. Personal devices may not be used to access source code or customer data.

## Security Training Devices

The mandatory security training is completed on the company laptop within the first week. Employees must keep the operating system and security software updated, use the password manager, and lock their screen when away from the device. Installing unapproved software requires an exception from IT Security.
