# Training Policy

This policy describes the mandatory and optional training available to employees during onboarding and beyond.

## Mandatory Security and Compliance Training

All employees must complete the security awareness, data protection and code-of-conduct modules within the first 5 working days of joining. The modules take about three hours in total and are taken on the company laptop through the learning portal. The training covers phishing, password hygiene, handling customer data, anti-bribery and anti-harassment rules. A score of at least 80 percent is required in each module; failed modules can be retaken twice. Access to production systems and customer data is withheld until the training is complete. Annual refresher training is mandatory for all employees, and HR reports completion rates to the compliance committee.

## Leadership Training for Managers

New people managers, whether hired externally or promoted, must complete the leadership programme within the first 30 days. It consists of four sessions on coaching, giving feedback, hiring and performance management, followed by a peer discussion group. The programme is scheduled by the Learning team and counts towards the manager's probation goals. New managers are also expected to set up recurring one-on-one meetings with each direct report within their first week and to introduce themselves to the team in a team meeting.

## Role-Specific Training

Each role has a short learning path agreed with the manager. Engineers set up their development environment, complete a code-review and release-process workshop in week one, and shadow an on-call rotation before joining it after 60 days. Sales employees complete CRM fundamentals and product training within their first two weeks. All role-specific training is tracked in the learning portal and discussed in the 30-day check-in.

## Learning Budget

Every full-time employee has an annual learning budget of 1,000 USD (or the local equivalent) for courses, books and conferences, plus two paid learning days per year. Requests are approved by the manager and submitted through the expenses system. Unused budget does not roll over. Employees in their probation period may use up to 50 percent of the budget.

## Intern Program

Interns follow a shorter onboarding programme. They receive a loaner laptop from the intern pool instead of custom hardware, join the cohort orientation instead of a personalised first-week schedule, and are paired with a mentor and a buddy. Interns complete the mandatory security training in their first week, attend weekly cohort sessions and present a project at the end of the internship. Interns are not enrolled in the Provident Fund or the 401(k) plan, and do not use the learning budget.
