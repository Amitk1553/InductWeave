# India Onboarding Policy

This policy applies to employees based in Mumbai, Bangalore and other Indian locations. It supplements the Global Onboarding Policy.

## Identity Documents (PAN and Aadhaar)

Every employee in India must submit a copy of their PAN card and their Aadhaar card to HR within 3 days of joining. The PAN is required for income-tax deduction at source; without it, tax is deducted at a higher rate. The Aadhaar number is needed for statutory verification and for linking your Provident Fund account. Upload clear colour scans through the HR portal; HR will verify the originals during orientation where possible. Documents submitted after the three-day window must be escalated to the HR Operations team, and payroll may be processed with the higher tax rate until the PAN is received. Passport-size photographs are collected on the first day for the ID badge.

## Provident Fund and UAN

Employees contribute to the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF). If you worked for a previous employer in India, provide your existing Universal Account Number (UAN) within 7 days of joining so that your new employer can link to the same account. If you do not have a UAN, HR generates one for you. Your UAN must be activated and linked with Aadhaar and your bank account to allow withdrawals and transfers later. The employer and employee each contribute 12 percent of basic salary. Interns are not enrolled in the Provident Fund.

## Bank Account Details

Salary is paid by direct bank transfer on the last working day of each month. Submit your bank account number, IFSC code and a cancelled cheque or recent bank statement within 3 days of joining. The account must be in your own name. Salary for the first month is paid in the next payroll cycle if the details arrive after the monthly payroll cut-off on the 20th.

## Background Verification

Employment in India is subject to successful background verification (BGV) carried out by our approved vendor. It covers identity, address, education and the last two employers. You will receive an email from the vendor with a consent form and should complete it before your start date; HR targets completion of BGV within five working days of the offer being accepted. Access to source code repositories and cloud environments is only granted after the BGV is cleared. Discrepancies found during verification are reviewed by HR and may affect the offer.

## Statutory Benefits and Leave (India)

In addition to the Global Leave Policy, employees receive Gratuity after five years of continuous service, and paid maternity leave of 26 weeks for the first two children as required by the Maternity Benefit Act; paternity leave of 10 working days is provided as a company benefit. Group medical insurance covers the employee, spouse, children and parents up to the sum insured shown in the benefits portal. Enrollment must be completed within 30 days of joining. Public holidays follow the company's published state-wise holiday list, with at least ten fixed holidays and two optional holidays per year.

## Working Hours (India)

Standard hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST, Monday to Friday. Employees in Bangalore and Mumbai work hybrid, with three office days a week. Employees working with US or UK teams may shift their hours by agreement with their manager, but should not exceed 48 hours per week. Night-shift allowances apply to employees who are formally rostered on night support.
