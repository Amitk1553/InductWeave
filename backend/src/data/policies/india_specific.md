# India Onboarding and Employment Policy

This policy outlines the statutory requirements, documentation procedures, and employment standards specific to employees of Nimbus Technologies India Private Limited operating in Mumbai and Bangalore.

## 3.1 Overview of India Operations

Nimbus Technologies India Private Limited operates development centres in Mumbai and Bangalore. All employment contracts in India are governed by local labour regulations, the Shops and Establishments Act, and applicable central statutory mandates. This policy supplements the Global Onboarding Policy with requirements specific to our Indian workforce.

## 3.2 Documents Required on Day One

All new employees joining our Mumbai or Bangalore offices must present specific original documents for in-person verification on their first day of work. Employees are required to bring:
- Permanent Account Number (PAN) card (original and photocopy).
- Aadhaar card (original and photocopy).
- Two (2) recent passport-sized color photographs against a white background.
- Highest education degree certificate and mark sheets (original and self-attested copies).
- Relieving letter and formal service certificate from the most recent prior employer.

Copies of all day-one documents must be uploaded to the Nimbus HR Portal. In particular, copies of the PAN card and Aadhaar card must be submitted within a strict deadline of three (3) business days from the start date. Failure to submit PAN details within 3 business days will cause tax deduction at the highest statutory rate under Indian tax regulations.

## 3.3 Provident Fund (PF) and UAN Registration

Employees are enrolled in the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) scheme in compliance with the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act:
- Employees with prior EPF membership must submit their existing 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN) to HR within seven (7) business days of joining.
- New hires without an existing UAN will have a fresh UAN generated by the Nimbus HR Operations team.
- The statutory contribution rate is 12% of the basic salary contributed by the employee, matched by an equal 12% employer contribution.
- Employees must ensure their UAN is linked with their Aadhaar and active bank account on the EPFO portal to facilitate seamless future transfers and withdrawals.

## 3.4 Bank Account Details and Salary Disbursement

Salaries at Nimbus Technologies India are paid on the final business day of each calendar month via direct electronic bank transfer:
- New hires must submit their Indian bank account details (account holder name, bank name, account number, and 11-character IFSC code) along with a cancelled cheque or recent bank statement.
- Bank details must be uploaded to the payroll portal within three (3) business days of the start date.
- The bank account must be an individual account held in the employee's legal name; joint accounts or non-resident accounts cannot be used for domestic payroll.
- Submissions received after the monthly payroll cutoff date (20th of the month) will have initial earnings disbursed in the subsequent regular payroll cycle.

## 3.5 Background Verification (BGV)

All offers of employment at Nimbus Technologies India are contingent upon successful completion of background verification conducted by our authorized screening agency:
- The background check verifies candidate identity, permanent address, highest academic credentials, criminal records, and employment history for the previous two employers.
- Candidates receive a secure digital consent link from the screening partner and must submit required supporting documents prior to their start date.
- The standard target turnaround time for background verification clearance is five (5) working days.
- Access to production source code repositories and cloud infrastructure environments is strictly withheld until formal BGV clearance is issued by HR.

## 3.6 India-Specific Leave and Statutory Benefits

Employees in India are entitled to statutory benefits under Indian labour statutes in addition to Nimbus global perks:
- Gratuity: Paid in accordance with the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 upon completion of five (5) years of continuous service.
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave for female employees for the first two surviving children, per the Maternity Benefit Amendment Act.
- Paternity Leave: 10 working days of fully paid paternity leave to be utilized within six months of childbirth.
- Public Holidays: 10 mandatory national and state holidays per year, plus two (2) optional floating festival holidays chosen from the company list.
